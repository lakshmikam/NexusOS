# OrbitDesk access and upgrade policy

Policy ID: access-v1
Version: 1
Effective from: 2026-10-01
Product: fictional OrbitDesk demo

## Subscription versus feature access

OrbitDesk Pro includes `team_workspace` and `advanced_reports`. Subscription status describes the current plan; entitlements describe which features are actually enabled. They can disagree when an access-provisioning problem occurs.

## Evidence for paid access

An active Pro subscription and a verified successful payment for the relevant invoice support investigating disabled Pro features. A pending payment alone does not establish paid access.

## Access correction and approval

A proposed entitlement correction must be checked against backend records and approved by an authorized operator before execution. Do not treat a model's recommendation as permission to change account state.
